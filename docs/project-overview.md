# 🏟️ K-HUB Sports Club Booking Platform
# Project Overview

---

## Project Name
K-HUB Sports Club Booking Platform

---

## Vision
Build a premium online sports club booking platform that delivers a seamless, modern, and enjoyable booking experience.
The platform should feel like a world-class SaaS product rather than a traditional local sports club website.

---

## Mission
Our mission is to simplify sports court booking by providing a fast, reliable, and intuitive platform where users can easily discover courts, check availability, reserve time slots, and manage their bookings.

---

## Problem Statement
Many sports clubs still rely on traditional booking methods such as:
- Phone calls
- WhatsApp messages
- Facebook Messenger
- Manual paper schedules

These methods often result in:
- Double bookings
- Slow response times
- Human errors
- Poor customer experience
- Difficult schedule management
- Limited booking visibility
- No real-time availability

---

## Solution
The platform provides a complete digital booking experience that allows users to:
- Browse sports courts
- Check real-time availability
- Reserve available time slots
- Manage personal bookings
- Explore memberships
- View events
- Discover official sponsors
- Contact the club

The experience should be simple, fast, and premium.

---

## Objectives
The platform aims to:
- Simplify the booking process.
- Eliminate duplicate bookings.
- Reduce manual booking management.
- Provide live availability.
- Deliver a premium user experience.
- Maintain high performance.
- Ensure responsiveness across all devices.

---

## Target Audience
The platform is designed for:
- Football Players
- Padel Players

- Club Members
- Families
- Visitors
- Sports Enthusiasts

---

## User Roles

### Guest
Guests can:
- Browse the website
- View courts
- Check availability
- Explore memberships
- View sponsors
- Browse events
- Contact the club

Guests cannot create bookings.

---

### Registered User
Registered users can:
- Book courts
- View booking history
- Manage bookings
- Update profile information
